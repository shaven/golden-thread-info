Golden Thread, shaped to the way you work

Pick one or more roles when you install. Each role changes three things: how gt writes drafts, briefs and summaries for you, which files every new project starts with, and the 12 one-tap buttons in your pane. Everything below is the real content of each role pack.

1 · How gt writes for you

Drafts, briefs and summaries in your language

The agents that write for you (drafts, briefs, summaries, wrap-ups) get this audience line. The agents that find and check facts never do, so your role changes how things are said, never what is recorded as true.
